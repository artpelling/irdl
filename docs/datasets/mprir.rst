MP-RIR contains 68,736 room impulse responses measured in a non-rectangular, furnished room. A robot moved a vertical array of eight omni-directional microphones (0.70 m to 1.75 m height, 15 cm spacing) to 1074 positions of a 15 cm grid. Eight loudspeakers of four types were measured: four subwoofers on the floor (S1 to S4) and four full-range loudspeakers on tables (S5 to S8).

Each call retrieves one loudspeaker, which is a separate 3.4 GB download, as a ``SingleRoomSRIR`` SOFA file with 1074 measurements and eight receivers. ``ListenerPosition`` is the foot of the microphone rod and ``ReceiverPosition`` holds the microphone heights, so their sum gives the provider's microphone coordinates. All coordinates are given in the room coordinate system with its origin at room corner C0. ``SourcePosition`` is the centre of the loudspeaker base.

By default, IRDL removes the guard time and the measurement system delay given in the provider's ``Setup.npz`` so that sample zero corresponds to sound emission; set ``remove_delay=False`` to keep the full responses and record the delay in ``Data.Delay``.
